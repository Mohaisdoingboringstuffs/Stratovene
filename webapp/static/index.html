STRATOVANE Thermal Model

The STRATOVANE model predicts the thermal behavior of a stratospheric electronics enclosure using a multi-node heat balance. It accounts for solar irradiance, atmospheric longwave radiation, and convective cooling in thin air.

This tool allows for the exploration of how different material coatings (e.g., AAO) affect the internal electronics temperature across various altitudes and scenarios.
